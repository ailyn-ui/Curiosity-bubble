# THE HIVE (v1, 2026-11-03)

Ailyn's business builder, and her map of how a business works. It is both:

- **The offer:** what clients buy. Ailyn finds the queen (the brand) and builds
  the hive around her.
- **The map:** how Ailyn thinks, diagnoses and plans. Every finding, tool and
  piece of content has a place in one of its cells.

Read alongside `positioning.md`. **Status: draft**, shaped with Ailyn on
2026-11-03; test it in the 10 listening conversations before treating it as final.

## The one line

> **Your brand is the queen. I build the hive around her.**

A hive works because every bee knows its job and the queen sits safely at the
centre. A founder with more ideas than capacity is trying to be every bee at once.
The Hive gives each job a cell, so the founder can go back to being the queen.

## The position (Ailyn, 2026-11-03)

The brand is at the core of every business. When it's clear and stated, it's
easier to market, sell, run and develop new things. And when operations,
finances, marketing and sales have help, a lot more gets done without spending
excess energy.

So the Hive has two halves: **make the brand clear**, then **give every cell help**.

## Who does what

**We build it together. She keeps it running. The help carries the work.**
Ailyn builds the hive with the founder, then hands it over; she never runs it
for her (the ongoing monthly role is ruled out in `positioning.md`). That edge
also protects Ailyn's own energy.

| Cell | We build together | She keeps it running | The help carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand, the queen | Her shape, voice, promise, story and look, found and written down | Lives it, approves it, is the face of it | Keeps everything consistent with it |
| Marketing and sales | A clear message, what to talk about, templates, an AI helper that writes in her voice | Her ideas, showing up, relationships, the sales conversations | Drafts, scheduling, follow-ups |
| Operations | How things are done and what only she knows, written down in one place; the tools set up | The decisions only she can make | Answers from what's written, so no one waits on her |
| Finances | Prices that make sense and a simple money structure (the Money Mirror) | A short money review, and the decisions | Bookkeeping and taxes (her bookkeeper or accountant) |
| Development | A place for her ideas, and a simple way to choose between them | Chooses what grows next | Researches and drafts the new idea |
| You | Boundaries and rhythms built into the setup from day one | Looks after her energy | The setup protects her time |
| Clients | The client journey, from first contact to referral | The relationship itself | Welcome emails, reminders, feedback requests |

The intensive finds the queen. The 40-day build builds the hive around her, cell
by cell, and ends with the handover. Ailyn confirmed on 2026-11-03 that the
"we build together" column fits what she can deliver in 40 days.

## The shape

Seven hexagons: the queen in the centre, six cells around her.

```
       Marketing & sales   Development
      Clients        BRAND        Operations
            You            Finances
```

## The queen: Brand

Everything else exists to serve and carry it.

| Sublevel | What it holds |
|---|---|
| One shape | what the business is, and what it isn't |
| Voice | how it sounds, in writing and out loud |
| Promise | what clients can count on, every time |
| Story | why you, why this, why now |
| Visual identity | name, marks, colours, type |

## The six cells

### 1. Marketing and sales: the foragers
*Bees that fly out and bring nectar home.* How the right people find you and say yes. Sales is part of this cell.

- Ideal client
- Message (what you say, in their words)
- Content and channels
- Referrals and partners
- Sales conversations

### 2. Operations: the comb builders
*Bees that build and keep the structure.* How the work gets done without everything going through you.

- Processes (how things are done, written down)
- Tools
- Client delivery
- Team and roles
- The brain: what only you know, in one clear place, so no one is waiting on you

### 3. Development: the nursery
*Where new bees are raised.* Where new things grow, on purpose.

- Offers and products
- The idea bank, and choosing between ideas
- Pricing structure
- Partnerships
- Learning

### 4. Finances: the honey stores
*What the hive saves for winter.* What the business keeps, and what it's safe to spend.

- Prices and margins
- Cash flow
- What's safe to spend
- Taxes and reserves
- Money reviews

### 5. You: temperature control
*Bees fan their wings to keep the hive at the right heat.* The founder's energy, capacity and limits.

- Energy (the body budget: what the business spends from you)
- Capacity (how much you can carry this season)
- Boundaries and non-negotiables
- Rhythms (how your weeks and seasons actually run)
- Support (who and what holds you)

### 6. Clients: the flowers
*The hive can't live without them, and they need the bees too.* Who you serve, and what they live through with you.

- Who they are (Sarah, Esti, the real clients)
- What they say, in their own words
- Their journey, from first contact to referral
- Results and stories
- Feedback

## How to use the map

- **Diagnosing a client:** go cell by cell. Where is the founder doing the bees' work herself? Which cell is empty, and which is overflowing?
- **Positioning and content:** every post, page and offer should say which cell it serves, and always return to the queen.
- **The brand brain:** organise what's written down by cell, so the founder and her team know where to look.
