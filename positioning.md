# Positioning (v1, 2026-10-31)

Worked out with the marketing board (`/marketing-board`) from Ailyn's real
client history. Everything else (website, offer, outreach, content, the
Message Mirror) should follow from this page. Supersedes the "brand, money &
the systems that hold them" framing in `website/home.md` where they differ.

## The statement

> **For women founders with more ideas than capacity**, years in, brilliant
> and scattered, and frustrated it hasn't taken off yet, **Ailyn is a
> one-to-one brand partner** who hears everything, including what isn't said,
> gives it one clear shape, and **contains it from day one** so it finally
> holds.
> **Unlike** doing it yourself with AI or working from a template, she builds
> the scope with you and keeps it in place.
> **Nine founders a year. 40 days each. Full attention.**

## Who

- Women founders with **more ideas than capacity**: overwhelmed, scattered,
  frustrated it hasn't taken off. Ailyn confirmed this describes her avatar.
- ADHD is **a way in, not a requirement** ("ADHD or not, a brain that never
  stops"). Past clients were mostly "scattered more than ADHD proper".
- Often at a **turning point**: outgrowing how they're seen, launching,
  pivoting.
- Buyer for the build = Esti level. Sarah gets free content and the Mirror.

## Why this position (evidence)

Five past clients, all found through Ailyn's network, none through Instagram:

| Client | What Ailyn did | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dalia, fullyinbalance.com (health coach) | All brand assets, programmes and more | First client, cheerleader, ~10 years in market |
| Lake You (swimwear brand) | Full brand assets and concept | Product brand |
| Chabad Zurich | Logo | In use for 12 years |
| Franzine → Franzine Media | Brand assets, strategy, quick rebrand | Podcast turned advisor for French-speaking expats in Zurich |
| One ADHD client | (stopped) | Very scattered; Ailyn stopped because she didn't contain the work from the start. Lesson: containment and non-negotiables from day one |

What clients got: they understood what they were selling, and had tools to
present themselves in a way they loved; marks and colours that let them
explain their brands and offers.

What clients said (Ailyn's recollection, not verbatim): an artist; incredibly
insightful; always great to work with; professional, organised, helpful,
available; a great listener, nothing escapes her; "witchy", connects things
deep inside, lives change.

## What only Ailyn brings

- Listens past the questionnaire: everything, including the non-verbal.
- Connects many ideas, organises them, shows what matters. Poetry,
  psychology and simplicity; MBA; lived in many places, studied many things;
  mom.
- Scope defined with the client, not from templates. Clear boundaries and
  non-negotiables for both sides.

## Competitive alternative

Founders now try it themselves with AI. AI only works with what you type;
Ailyn hears what isn't said.

## Business model

- 9 clients a year, 40 days each, CHF 9,000-15,000, full VIP attention.
- Entry by conversation only; non-negotiables stated before signing.
- Previously charged ~1,000, years ago; no one knows, so no price increase to
  explain. Brief past clients on the new offer before asking for referrals.
- Option (undecided): first one or two clients at a stated founding price in
  exchange for a case study.
- Languages: English first; Spanish later as a possible second market.
- No client yet this year; coming back is part of the story.

## What follows from it

- **Website:** lead with "more ideas than capacity" and the listening; proof
  high (12-year logo, podcast to business); ADHD in how she works and her story.
- **Outreach:** people, not reach. Past clients first (testimonials, then
  Dalia's introductions); Zurich partners (Franzine Media, women-founder and
  expat networks); founders visibly frustrated or at a turning point. About 3
  good conversations a month gets to 9 clients a year.
- **The Message Mirror:** unchanged; the conversation opener. The "no pitch"
  follow-up is where people experience the listening.
- **Content:** shows the thinking and the work; supports referrals rather than
  being the main channel.
