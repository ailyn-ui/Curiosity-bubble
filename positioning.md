## The statement

> **For women founders with more ideas than capacity**, and now AI giving
> them even more ideas and drafts that don't sound like them, **Ailyn listens
> until she finds your one real shape and voice**, gives it form, and **sets up
> your brand and your AI to hold it**.
>
> The only brand partner who listens until she finds your real shape and
> voice, then builds the AI that keeps it.

- **One word:** shape. Voice is how the shape shows up.
- **The pain:** scattered, overwhelmed, years in, frustrated it hasn't taken off.
- **Today's reality:** AI multiplies ideas and output, but it can't choose
  between them, and what it writes doesn't sound like her.
- **Why AI matters in the offer:** there's no ongoing role, so the AI Ailyn
  sets up is what keeps the founder in her shape after the work ends.
  Containment from day one, continued after Ailyn leaves.

## Who

- Women founders with **more ideas than capacity**: overwhelmed, scattered,
  frustrated it hasn't taken off. Ailyn confirmed this describes her avatar.
- Already using AI for the business, or about to, and finding the output
  generic or "not me".
- ADHD is **a way in, not a requirement** ("ADHD or not, a brain that never
  stops"). Past clients were mostly "scattered more than ADHD proper".
- Timing signal: a **turning point** (outgrowing how they're seen, launching,
  pivoting) is when they start looking.
- Buyer for the offers = Esti level. Sarah gets free content and the Mirror.

## Not for

- Founders who want assets fast and cheap.
- Founders who want someone to run their content every month.
- Founders who aren't willing to choose between their ideas.

available; a great listener, nothing escapes her; "witchy", connects things
deep inside, lives change.

## What only Ailyn brings

- Listens past the questionnaire: everything, including the non-verbal.
- Connects many ideas, organises them, shows what matters. Poetry,
  psychology and simplicity; MBA; lived in many places, studied many things;
  mom.
- Scope defined with the client, not from templates. Clear boundaries and
  non-negotiables for both sides.
- Builds AI helpers herself (this repo: client avatars, Instagram tools, the
  Money Mirror spreadsheet).

## Competitive alternatives

1. AI first: ChatGPT / Claude for strategy and copy, Canva / Looka for visuals.
2. Courses and templates.
3. A cheap freelance designer.
4. A business coach.
5. An agency rebrand.
6. Nothing: keep going, keep explaining.

AI only works with what you type; Ailyn hears what isn't said, chooses, and
sets up the AI to keep it.
