# THE APIARY (draft v1, 2026-11-03)

The whole of a founder's world, the way Ailyn builds it: her business and her
home, side by side, connected but never merged. An apiary is the place where a
beekeeper keeps her hives. **The founder is the beekeeper.** She tends both hives
and decides what each one gets; she stops doing the bees' work in either.

Read with `hive.md` (the business hive in detail) and `positioning.md`.
**Status: draft, thinking out loud.** Names and offers can still change.

## The four parts

```
                 THE LANDING BOARD
          (one place to put everything in)
                 /                \
        THE HIVE                    HOME
   (the business:            (her private side:
    brand + six cells)        calendar, tasks, and more if she wants)
             \                    /
              ME  ←—— doorway ——→
        (only what she chooses passes)
```

### 1. The landing board: one way in
In a real hive, every bee arrives on the landing board at the entrance. Here,
everything lands in one place: voice notes, quick texts, photos, forwarded
emails, half-thoughts at 11pm. She never has to decide where a thought belongs.

**AI sorts it** into the right place: the brand, one of the six business cells,
or Home.

| What she drops in | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| "Idea for a workshop" | Hive → Development |
| "Client asked for the invoice" | Hive → Finances |
| "New testimonial from a client" | Hive → Clients |
| "Dentist Thursday at 3" | Home → calendar; the Hive only sees "not available Thursday 3–4" |
| "Buy football boots before Saturday" | Home → tasks |

**The safety rule: when the AI isn't sure, it keeps the item private.** Anything
unclear goes to Home, never to the business side where the team can see it.

**She stays in charge:** a short weekly look at what was sorted, to move anything
that landed in the wrong place. The sorting learns from her corrections.

### 2. The Hive: the business
The brand is the queen at the centre, with six cells around it: Marketing and
sales, Operations, Development, Finances, Me, Clients. Her team can come in.
Full detail in `hive.md`.

**We build it together. She keeps it running. The help carries the work.**

### 3. Home: her private side
Operational, like the Hive, but it depends much more on her: there's no team to
carry it, so it has to stay light enough to keep running on a tired week.

- **Starts small:** her **calendar** and her **tasks**. That's the minimum, and it's
  enough for the Hive to finally know her real capacity.
- **Grows if she wants:** ideas and personal projects, household money, home admin
  and requirements.
- **Private by default.** The team never enters Home.

Name: **Home** for now (the Nest was the other option).

### 4. Me: the doorway
"Me" is the cell in the Hive that holds her energy, capacity, boundaries, rhythms
and support. It's also the doorway between the two hives. Only what she chooses
passes through, for example "not available Friday afternoon", never why.

What passes between them:

| From Home to the Hive | From the Hive to Home |
|---|---|
| Her capacity this week and this season | Deadlines and launches that will need energy |
| Her non-negotiables (pickup at 3:30, Fridays off) | What she earned, and what's safe to spend |
| When she does her best thinking | Busy and quiet seasons coming up |
| What she wants the business to make possible | What the team needs from her, and when |
