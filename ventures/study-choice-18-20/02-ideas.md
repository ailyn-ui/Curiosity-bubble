## Top three

**1. The Major Decision Sprint (47)**
- *Buyer:* the parent, or the 18 to 20 year old themselves. *User:* the 18 to 20 year old.
- *Promise:* in 14 days, go from "I have no idea" to a shortlist you've actually tested and a choice you can explain to anyone, including your parents.
- *Why it wins:* it's the only idea backed by direct price evidence (workbooks at $18–20 on Payhip and Thinkific, plus the Pearson textbook). It also fills the clearest gap: free tests stop at "your type", and this picks up exactly there.
- *What would kill it:* it reads like yet another generic worksheet. It has to feel like a sprint with daily tasks, not a reflection journal.

**2. Test-Drive a Degree (46)**
- Strong on the "what's the job really like" pain and well-supported in research. Its weakness is that we have less evidence people *pay* for experiment kits.

**3. AI-Resilience Check (46)**
- The most timely pain (2026 surveys). Two risks: claims about the future can age badly, and overpromising is a liability. It must be framed as a *method for thinking it through*, not a prediction. It makes an excellent **free lead magnet** rather than the main product.

## Recommendation

**Build idea 1, the Major Decision Sprint, and fold ideas 2 and 3 into it:**
- days 1–4: narrow down, starting from any free test result they already have
- days 5–10: test-drive the top 3 fields (idea 2)
- days 11–12: AI-resilience check on the finalists (idea 3)
- days 13–14: decide, plus a one-page decision brief for the parent conversation (idea 7)

Give a free 10-question version of the AI-resilience check away to build the email list.

**Pricing:** $29 for the student edition and $39 for a parent bundle that adds a "how to help without pushing" guide. That sits above the $18–20 workbooks because it actually gets you to a decision, and far below a $200+ consultant.

## Validation test before building it in full (2 weeks)

1. A one-page waitlist site with a "founding price $19" pre-sale on Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy, plus the free AI-resilience check as the opt-in.
2. Find traffic **without spamming communities**. Most of the subreddits ban self-promotion, so answer questions with real help and keep the link on your profile only where the rules allow. Add 10–15 short TikToks or Shorts on "is my major AI-proof?". Pitch to 10 school or college counsellors.
3. **Pass if:** at least 150 email sign-ups and at least 10 pre-sales. **Rethink if:** under 50 sign-ups (reach problem) or sign-ups but zero sales (the free check is enough for them, so sell to parents instead).

## Notes

- Switzerland and German-speaking countries come later: free cantonal counselling makes people less willing to pay.
- Every product needs a short mental-health note pointing to professional support, because anxiety and depression come up often in this audience.
