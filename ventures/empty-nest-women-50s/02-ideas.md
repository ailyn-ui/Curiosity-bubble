## Top three

**1. The Next Chapter Plan (51)**
- *Buyer and user:* the woman herself.
- *Promise:* in 6 weeks, go from "I don't know who I am without a full house" to a written plan for the next ten years: what you'll do with your days, your marriage, your money and your work. No religion, no fluff, one small action every day.
- *Why it wins:* the most-repeated pain, and the clearest gaps. The bottom of the market is cheap journals about feelings, a large share of what exists is faith-based, and a university charges $295 for structure. Nothing structured, practical and self-paced exists at $97–149.
- *What would kill it:* it reads like therapy or a gratitude journal. Every week has to end with a **decision or a real-world experiment** (a class, a conversation, a trial project), not a reflection.

**2. Mothering Adults (47)**
- Fast to build, very shareable, and an easy first purchase. It works best as the **entry product** ($19–29) that leads into idea 1.

**3. Almost Empty (43)**
- It reaches her a year earlier, through the biggest community (Grown and Flown, parents of 15–25 year olds), and moves her into idea 1 once the child leaves.

## Recommendation

**Build idea 1, The Next Chapter Plan.** Six weeks, each ending with a decision:
1. Who am I now? Values, energy, what was put on hold.
2. Mothering adults: the new relationship (idea 2, condensed).
3. The marriage, or life on your own (idea 5, condensed).
4. Work and purpose: three small experiments.
5. Money: know your numbers, education only, with a pointer to a qualified adviser.
6. The ten-year plan: one page.

**Product ladder:** Mothering Adults at $24 → The Next Chapter Plan at $129 (founding price $79) → an optional monthly group call at $29/month later.

## Validation test (2 weeks)

1. A waitlist page with a founding pre-sale at $79, plus a free lead magnet: *"The 10-question 'What's Next For Me' check-in."*
2. Traffic: be genuinely helpful in Grown and Flown and similar groups, following their rules (no link drops). Pitch 5–10 midlife podcasts as a guest on "the practical side of an empty nest". Run Pinterest pins, since this audience uses Pinterest heavily (*assumption, not verified in this research*).
3. **Pass if:** at least 200 sign-ups and at least 10 pre-sales. **Rethink if:** sign-ups but no sales (test idea 2 at $24 as the first purchase instead), or under 50 sign-ups (a reach problem: try podcasts before groups).

## Notes for the user

- **Overlap with your own audience:** many of these women are moms, and some will be founders, close to Sarah and Esti. The separate brand keeps the two apart. Decide up front whether you're fine with the products serving a neighbouring audience, and don't cross-promote unless you choose to.
- The money and menopause topics stay educational, with clear pointers to professionals.
- Mental health: include a short note pointing to professional support, since loneliness and low mood come up in this audience.
