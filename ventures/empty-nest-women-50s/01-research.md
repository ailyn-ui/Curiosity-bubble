# Research: women in their 50s whose children are leaving home

- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Market:** English-speaking (US and UK), with a short note on Switzerland at the end
- **Method:** web search only. Opening pages directly was blocked in this session (reddit.com and other hosts), so the quotes are **search excerpts** that haven't been checked against the original pages.
- **Searches run (11):** identity and purpose; prevalence and menopause studies; existing products and prices; career reinvention after 50; marriage and grey divorce; relationships with adult children; Etsy bestsellers; the Grown and Flown community; retirement-savings worries; complaints in book reviews; prices of career-relaunch programs

## Top pains (ranked)

1. **"Who am I when I'm not needed every day?"** Identity and purpose.
   Seen everywhere: blogs, podcasts, essays, faith sites. Intensity: high.
   - > "I spent twenty two years putting my kids first in every decision I made and now that they're grown I'm sitting in an empty house realizing I have no idea who I am when I'm not somebody's mother" (essay title, [Artful Parent](https://artfulparent.com/a-bt-i-spent-twenty-two-years-putting-my-kids-first-in-every-decision-i-made-and-now-that-theyre-grown-im-sitting-in-an-empty-house-realizing-i-have-no-idea-who-i-am-when-im-not-somebodys-mother/))
   - Women each "expressed some variation of 'Who am I' and 'What am I meant to do with the rest of my life?'" ([Grown and Flown](https://grownandflown.com/children-grown-what-is-my-purpose/), excerpt)
   - Moms say they've "lost the best job I've ever had" ([Encore Living podcast](https://buzzsprout.com/2102555/episodes/16964399-empty-nest-life-why-you-feel-lost-and-why-that-s-totally-normal-149?t=0), excerpt)
   - **Counterweight:** the clinical evidence is mixed. A small study found empty nest syndrome rare among depressed midlife women ([atomi](https://atomi.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/578/files/atomi-KJ00005663749.pdf)), and marital satisfaction can *rise* once the nest is empty ([Psychology Today](https://psychologytoday.com/ie/blog/adult-development-through-the-lifespan/201908/is-empty-nest-syndrome-real)). Many women feel freedom as well as loss: "deep pride... and still an ache". **So the angle is "what's next", not "grief".**

2. **The marriage: "Who is this person across the table?"**
   Intensity: high.
   - Grey divorce among couples over 50 has doubled in two decades while overall divorce fell ([Psychology Today](https://psychologytoday.com/ie/blog/adult-development-through-the-lifespan/201908/is-empty-nest-syndrome-real), via search). 34% of grey divorces end marriages of 30+ years (Pew, via [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/gray-divorce-why-older-couples-181754722.html)).
   - Couples who were very child-focused struggle most ([tovima](https://www.tovima.com/society/when-the-nest-empties-what-happens-to-the-marriage/amp/), [Focus on the Family](https://www.focusonthefamily.ca/content/redefining-your-marriage-after-the-nest-empties-out)). Coverage from 2026 shows older adults "no longer staying in empty shell" marriages ([Las Vegas Sun](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2026/jun/29/older-adults-are-no-longer-staying-in-empty-shell/), [AJC](https://www.ajc.com/wellness/2026/05/older-couples-are-rethinking-marriage-and-considering-a-gray-divorce/)).
   - *Unverified:* "40% more likely to divorce after kids leave". The citation couldn't be confirmed, so don't use it.

3. **Mothering adults: how much to text, advise, help.**
   There's a lot of advice content and many advice-column letters. Intensity: medium-high.
   - Unsolicited advice is the main friction point ([YourTango](https://yourtango.com/family/boundaries-adult-children-setting-previous-generations-didnt), [Crosswalk](https://www.crosswalk.com/slideshows/10-tips-for-navigating-boundaries-with-your-adult-children.html)). A mom writes in to [Ask Eric](https://www.startribune.com/ask-eric-mom-worried-about-being-overbearing/601436569) worried about being overbearing. Woman's World ran "Help me get along with my adult children" ([PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/usa/womans-world/20240408/282475713801095)).
   - Grown and Flown now runs a whole section for parents of post-college adults ([link](https://grownandflown.com/post-college/)).

4. **Money: catching up for retirement.**
   Intensity: high, **but it's regulated territory.**
   - UK, 2023, n=2,009 employed 50–59 year olds: 66% of women don't know how much they've saved, 60% worry they aren't saving enough, and 48% don't expect to afford a moderate retirement. On average men have £17,014 more saved ([TPT](https://www.tpt.co.uk/news-insights/two-thirds-of-women-in-their-50s-don-t-know-how-much-is-saved-in-their-pension)).
   - US, older: only 25% of women expect retirement to be better than their working years ([Motley Fool, 2018](https://www.fool.com/retirement/2018/03/06/only-1-in-4-women-thinks-life-will-be-better-in-re.aspx)).

5. **Work and reinvention: a second act, often their own business.**
   Intensity: medium-high.
   - Self-employment among UK women over 50 is rising, driven by redundancy, caring duties and wanting control over their hours ([Prowess](https://prowess.org.uk/how-to-reinvent-yourself-post-50/)).
   - *Unverified:* AARP 2022 says women over 50 are the fastest-growing group of entrepreneurs (via [Prowess](https://prowess.org.uk/reinventing-yourself/)).
   - Relaunch programs exist and charge $295–375 ([Fox 35](https://www.fox35orlando.com/news/new-program-helps-women-relaunch-their-careers), [News4Jax](https://news4jax.com/news/morning-show/2024/08/07/relaunch-revolution-moms-reclaim-their-careers)).

6. **Menopause happening at the same time.** It's often mentioned alongside the rest ([SciELO review](https://www.scielo.br/j/ramb/a/9sTryWQbrMmLpGRWcxKBBgF/)), but the link is weakly evidenced and it's medical, so **don't build on it**. Just acknowledge it.

## Gaps and complaints

- **Much of the existing market is Christian.** Crosswalk, iBelieve, Focus on the Family, Jill Savage and faith podcasts lead the search results. A Goodreads reviewer: "4.5 stars for empty nest content minus 1.5 stars for the overwhelming religious themes" ([Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/3188397), excerpt). → There's room for a **secular, practical** option.
- Most products are about *feelings* (journals, reflection). Few help her **decide and do** something: work, marriage, money, time.
- A NetGalley reader had "hoped for some encouragement and maybe a little direction" and didn't get it ([NetGalley](https://www.netgalley.com/book/82472/reviews), excerpt).

## Who pays

She does. The person with the problem is also the buyer. Her time and budget may have just freed up (no more kids at home, possibly no more tuition), but pain 4 shows money worry too. **Position the product as an investment in the next 30 years, not a treat.**

## Where they gather

- The **Grown and Flown** Facebook group: self-reported at 125k–270k members, parents of 15–25 year olds ([Katie Couric](https://katiecouric.com/lifestyle/2832-2), [Grown and Flown](https://grownandflown.com/grown-and-flown-about-us/)). This is the largest single place.
- Podcasts: *Beyond the Empty Nest*, *Encore Living*, *The Intentional Midlife Mom*, *Momma's Motivational Messages* (Gen X).
- Sites: Sixty and Me, Next Avenue, Substack newsletters (*The Midst*, *The After Party*).
- Reddit: subreddits such as r/emptynest and r/Mid_Life are suggested in search results but **weren't checked**.

## Switzerland and German-speaking countries
