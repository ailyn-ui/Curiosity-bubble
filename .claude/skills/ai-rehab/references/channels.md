# Channel notes

Guess the channel from the draft, say the guess on the card, and follow its notes. "For email" and the other channel phrases override the guess.

## LinkedIn post
- Short paragraphs are normal, but not a wall of one-liners. Join related lines.
- Keep the writer's hashtags. Remove them only on "drop hashtags" or when the instructions in force say so.
- First line: the point or a concrete moment, not a drumroll.

## Instagram caption
- Line breaks and emojis are normal. Remove emoji bullets and slogan triads, and keep the other emojis unless the user says "drop emojis".
- Keep the writer's hashtags as written.
- Keep the offer details exact: price, date, length, booking step.

## Email to a client or prospect
- Keep the greeting, the thanks, and any softened ask at the writer's level of courtesy.
- Replace a canned opener ("I hope this email finds you well") with a specific line from the draft, such as thanks for the meeting it mentions.
- Keep every term: price, dates, scope, names. End with the real next step.

## Newsletter
- One idea, told with the draft's own scene or detail.
- Cut the summary bow at the end.

## Sales page
- Every price, deadline, guarantee, condition, and limit stays word for word in meaning. "Unless", "only", and "up to" carry the offer.
- Replace hype with what the buyer gets and when.

## Bio or About page
- Keep years, clients, places, and roles. Drop praise words.

## Slack, DMs, texts
- Keep it short and casual. Fix chatbot leftovers and leave the writer's shorthand alone.

## Any other channel
Job posts, case studies, ads, event recaps, business-listing posts, product pages, and anything else not listed above follow one rule. Work out who reads it and what they will do next (apply, buy, book, show up, hire, call). Every detail they act on stays exact: pay, dates, times, places, requirements, specs, results and their numbers, and how to take the next step. Keep the format those readers expect, such as a job post's sections or a product page's spec list, and rehab the sentences inside it.
