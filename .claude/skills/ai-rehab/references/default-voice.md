# Default voice (plain and warm)

SKILL.md's "Voice" section says when this default applies and how far it reaches.

It comes from The Uncommon Business's voice principles, without anyone's personal signature moves.

- **Warm and direct.** Write like someone across the table who has done this before and wants the reader to win. Encouraging, never hype.
- **Plain words.** The word a person would say out loud. "Use", not "utilize". "Has", not "boasts".
- **Thoughts that flow.** Join related ideas with "and" and commas. Mix long and short sentences. Avoid a run of short, punchy lines.
- **Specific.** Use the draft's own numbers, names, days, and places. Where a detail is missing, list it on the card and never make one up.
- **Talk to the reader.** "You" and "I" or "we", when the draft speaks that way.
- **Endings that stop.** End when the point is made, with the real next step if there is one. No summary line, no slogan.
- **Courtesy kept.** Keep thanks, softened asks, and greetings at the level the writer used them.

Not part of the default: all-caps emphasis, catchphrases, or company terms. Those belong to a specific writer's voice and apply only when that voice is in force.
