# Demand research playbook

You're looking for three kinds of signal, from strongest to weakest:

1. **Money**: they already pay for something like it (sales, reviews, prices).
2. **Pull**: they ask for it unprompted ("is there a template for...",
   "how do you all handle...").
3. **Pain**: they complain about the problem but haven't asked for a fix.

An idea backed only by pain is a hypothesis. Money plus pull is a strong
idea.

## Sources and search patterns

Swap in the audience (`A`) and the problem words you learn along the way.
Search in the audience's own language and market if it isn't English.

### Communities (pain + pull)
- `site:reddit.com A "struggling with"`, `site:reddit.com A "is there a template"`,
  `site:reddit.com A "what do you use for"`, `site:reddit.com A "I wish"`
- Find the audience's subreddits first (`A subreddit`), then search inside
  them for "template", "checklist", "spreadsheet", "course", "recommend".
- Forums, Quora, Mumsnet / BabyCenter / niche forums, Facebook group names
  (the posts are usually closed, but group names and sizes show where people
  gather), Discord servers.
- YouTube: comments under the top videos for the audience's main problem.

### Marketplaces (money)
- Etsy: `site:etsy.com A template`, `A planner`, `A printable`. Note price,
  review count (a rough proxy for sales) and "bestseller" badges.
- Gumroad Discover, Payhip, Stan stores: `site:gumroad.com A`.
- Amazon books and KDP: workbooks and journals for A. 1 to 3 star reviews
  are a goldmine for gaps.
- Udemy / Skillshare / Domestika: course titles, enrollments, ratings, the
  complaints in reviews.
- Notion template galleries, Canva template sellers, Teachers Pay Teachers
  (education), Creative Market (creative), AppSumo (business tools).

### Search demand (pull)
- Google "People also ask" and autocomplete phrasing: `how to A ...`,
  `A checklist`, `A template`, `best A planner`.
- Google Trends, if reachable, for direction (rising or falling).
- Search-volume tools usually can't be reached; don't guess numbers. Say
  "volume not checked" if you have none.

### Competitors (gaps)
- For the top 3 to 5 existing products: what they promise, price, format,
  and what reviewers say is missing or annoying.

## How much is enough

Aim for about 25 to 50 sources across at least three source types before
scoring. Stop earlier if the same pains keep repeating (saturation). Keep
going if every source says something new.

## Logging format (01-research.md)

```
# Research: <audience>
Date, market/language, searches run (list)

## Top pains (ranked)
1. <pain> — seen N times across <sources> — intensity: high/med/low
   > "verbatim quote" (link)

## What they already buy
| Product | Platform | Price | Reviews/sales signal | Format | Link |

## Gaps and complaints about existing products
## Where they gather (with size where visible)
## Unverified / couldn't reach
```

Quotes are verbatim, with their link. Never paraphrase a quote and present it
as theirs. Leave out usernames and personal details; the content of the post
is what matters.
