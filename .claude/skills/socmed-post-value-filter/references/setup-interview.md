# Setup Interview (shared)

This setup is shared by two skills, **Hook Engine** and **Post Value Filter**. Both read the same `CONTENT PROFILE` block, so someone who installs both only does this once.

The goal is a profile sharp enough that "generic" means something. A hook written for "a bookkeeper talking to restaurant owners who dread month-end" can be specific. A hook written for "business owners" can't.

## How to run it

Open with one short line: "Two minutes of setup so everything I write or score is tuned to your audience. Nine quick questions, answer as loosely as you like."

Ask **one or two questions at a time**, conversationally. Don't dump all nine at once. If an answer is vague, push once with the follow-up listed, then accept what you get and move on.

If the user shares a website, bio, sales page, or a few past posts, read them first and pre-fill what you can. Then ask only about the gaps.

## The nine questions

**1. What do you do, and who do you do it for?**
One or two sentences. Example: "I'm a bookkeeper for restaurant owners doing $1M to $5M a year."
Push if vague: "If you could only work with one type of client, who would it be?"

**2. Describe your ideal reader on a bad day.**
What's going wrong, what has she already tried, and what does she say to herself about it?
Push if vague: "What's the exact sentence she'd type into Google, or say to a friend at 11pm?"

**3. What does she say, word for word?**
Phrases your clients use in DMs, sales calls, reviews, or intake forms. Paste real messages if you have them (remove names). Her exact words make the best hooks.

**4. What does she want instead?**
The outcome in her words. "Stop dreading Sunday nights," not "achieve work-life integration."

**5. What do you know that most people in your space get wrong or never say?**
Contrarian takes, the mistakes you see constantly, the mechanism behind the problem.
Push if vague: "What advice in your industry makes you roll your eyes?"

**6. What real proof can you use?**
Only true things you're willing to share publicly: your own results, client results (with permission), credentials, years in business, number of clients served, and personal stories (a low point, a turning point, a mistake). "None yet" is a perfectly good answer, because plenty of strong hooks need no proof at all.
Tell them: "I'll never make up numbers or stories for you. Whatever you list here is the only proof I'll use."

**7. What do you sell, and what's the next step you want readers to take?**
Main offer plus the usual CTA (DM a word, link in bio, book a call, join the list, or nothing).

**8. How do you sound?**
Three words for your voice, plus one line you've written that sounds exactly like you. Include any habits (emojis, swearing, slang, all lowercase).

**9. What should I never say, and where do you post?**
Banned words, claims you legally can't make (health, finance, and income claims especially), off-limits topics, phrases you hate, and your platforms.

## The quick start (when they want output now)

If there's no profile and they want hooks or a verdict right away, ask only these three in a single message:

> Quick three so this fits your audience:
> 1. Who is this for? (one sentence)
> 2. What's her biggest frustration right now?
> 3. Any real result, number, or story I can use? ("None" is fine.)

Do the work, then add one line at the end: "Want the full two-minute setup so everything from here is sharper?"

## Handing over the profile

When the interview is done, write the profile in exactly this format. Keep each line tight, and use their words where possible.

```
CONTENT PROFILE
(used by Hook Engine and Post Value Filter)
Business: [what they do + for whom]
Ideal reader: [who she is, specifically]
Her bad day: [the struggle, what she's tried, what she says to herself]
Her words: [2-5 exact phrases clients use]
What she wants: [the outcome, in her words]
What I know that others miss: [2-4 contrarian takes, mechanisms, or common mistakes]
My real proof: [true results, credentials, and stories I'm willing to share, or "none yet"]
Offer + usual CTA: [what they sell + the next step they ask for]
Voice: [3 words] · Sounds like: "[their sample line]"
Never say: [banned words, claims, topics]
Platforms: [where they post]
Post Value Filter mode: [fix / coach / ask each time]
```

Then tell them how to save it so they never repeat setup. Offer these in order:

1. **Best: a Claude Project.** Create a Project for your content (or open the one you already use), paste the block into the Project instructions, and do your content work inside that Project. Both skills will find it automatically.
2. **Everywhere: your profile preferences.** Paste it into the personal preferences field in Claude's Settings so it loads in every chat.
3. **Simplest: a note.** Save it in your notes app and paste it at the start of any chat where you want to use it.

Finish with one line that fits the skill that ran setup: "You're set. Tell me what you need hooks for." or "You're set. Paste a post or an idea and I'll run it."

## Updating the profile

If they say "update my content profile," add new proof, or say something that contradicts the profile ("my audience is mostly men now"), rewrite the full block with the change and remind them to replace their saved copy.

## Older profiles

If you find a block titled `POST VALUE FILTER PROFILE`, treat it as a Content Profile. Use it as is, and ask the three questions it's missing (her words, real proof, platforms) only when the task needs them.
