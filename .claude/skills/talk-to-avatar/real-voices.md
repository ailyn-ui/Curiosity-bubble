## 2026-11-02 – listening conversation #1 (written answers, verbatim)
context: health and nutrition coach, nearly 10 years in business, past client
of Ailyn (warm, likely kind bias). Uses AI daily. Moms and household come first.

On what finally made her website clear:
> "It looked beautiful, but they still asked me what the programs were. It took
> me a while to see that the pages needed to sound like how my clients talk
> about their own struggles (the 3pm crash, the post Yom Tov spiral), and then
> the clarity came."

On why now:
> "I was coming up on 10 years in business and saw that my website still
> described who I was years ago." · "wanted to feel VIP so prices would be
> reflected VIP"

On what she tried and what it cost:
> "I hired a Fiverr freelancer to get the first draft of the website copy. Then
> Ailyn reviewed it and gave me feedback, and once I had the right messaging, I
> did the revisions myself with AI. It cost me about $150 and about a year
> without pressure"

On AI:
> "AI is helpful for getting a first draft down fast and for tightening copy
> once I know the message. I still review everything, because it doesn't sound
> personal until I've put my own voice, my client stories, and the Jewish
> calendar context into it."

On what almost stopped her:
> "Worrying that AI would make my words sound generic, and not knowing which
> tools were worth my time." · "It can be overwhelming, too many skills, GPTs
> and dashboards"

Her sorted day:
> "AI skills run in the background (I still check them), with protected focus
> time for email marketing, my main lead gen tool. Extra projects and ideas fit
> in the gaps"
