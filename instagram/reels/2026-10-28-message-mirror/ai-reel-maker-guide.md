# AI Reel Maker Guide – The Message Mirror (Halloween)

A ready-to-use brief for any AI video tool (Runway, Kling, Pika, Sora, CapCut, Canva, Captions, etc.).
Copy the **Global settings** into the tool once, then generate each **scene** separately and join them in order.

---

## 1. Global settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Format | Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920 |
| Total length | ~11–12 seconds, 6 scenes |
| Style | Cinematic, moody Halloween, elegant, not gory or cartoonish |
| Palette | Scenes 1–4: dark, candlelit, black and deep shadow with warm flicker. Scenes 5–6: bright, clean, white |
| Brand colours | Black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, pink accent ≈ `#E6005C` (swap in your exact brand pink) |
| Font for text | Bold serif, capitals (Playfair Display or similar) |
| Upload as asset | `cover.png` (The Message Mirror cover), used in scenes 4–6 |
| Voiceover | Warm, calm, female, unhurried, slightly mysterious in scenes 1–3, brighter in 5–6. Or record your own voice (recommended) |
| Music | Soft, playful-spooky music box or low strings, building to a light, bright note at scene 5 |

**Style prompt to paste into the tool (if it has a "global style" box):**
> Cinematic vertical video, moody Halloween atmosphere, candlelight, an ornate antique mirror, elegant black and white with magenta-pink accents, soft film grain, shallow depth of field, tasteful and stylish, not scary or gory.

**Negative prompt (if the tool supports one):**
> gore, blood, skulls, monsters, jump scares, cartoon style, cluttered background, distorted hands, distorted faces, extra fingers, garbled text, watermarks

## 2. Scene by scene

> **Tip:** AI video tools often scramble written words. Generate scenes **without text**, then add all on-screen text and the cover in the editor (CapCut, Canva, Instagram's editor).

### Scene 1 – The hook (0:00–0:02, 2s)
- **Voiceover:** "What if it wasn't you?"
- **On-screen text:** MIRROR, MIRROR… IS IT ME?
- **Video prompt:**
  > A woman in her 30s, soft dark clothing, steps toward an ornate antique gold mirror in a dark room lit by flickering candles. Her reflection moves a split-second behind her. Slow push-in camera. Moody, cinematic, Halloween atmosphere.
- **Must:** movement from the very first frame. No static start.

### Scene 2 – The gap (0:02–0:04, 2s)
- **Voiceover:** "Maybe there's a gap"
- **On-screen text:** THERE'S A GAP
- **Video prompt:**
  > Close-up: the woman holds up a small white card toward the antique mirror, candlelight flickering. Cobwebs on the mirror's corner, a carved pumpkin glowing softly in the background, out of focus.
- **Add in editor:** on her card, the words *Full brand + structure* (clean serif, black).

### Scene 3 – What is heard (0:04–0:06, 2s)
- **Voiceover:** "between what you say and what is heard."
- **On-screen text:** WHAT YOU SAY ≠ WHAT THEY HEAR (the ≠ in pink)
- **Video prompt:**
  > Close-up of the reflection in the antique mirror. The card in the reflection looks different and slightly warped, the mirror glass rippling like water. Candle flames flicker. Eerie but elegant.
- **Add in editor:** on the reflected card, *a logo?* in smaller, drippy Halloween lettering.

### Scene 4 – The reveal (0:06–0:08, 2s)
- **Voiceover:** "So I made The Message Mirror."
- **On-screen text:** none (the cover carries its own title)
- **Video prompt:**
  > The antique mirror's glass flickers and shimmers with light, then turns clear. Magical, glowing transition. Candles in the foreground.
- **Add in editor:** place `cover.png` inside the mirror frame as the glass clears.

### Scene 5 – Clarity (0:08–0:10, 2s)
- **Voiceover:** "A quick tool to get clear."
- **On-screen text:** A TOOL FOR CLARITY (black on white, "CLARITY" in pink)
- **Video prompt:**
  > The candles blow out with a soft puff of smoke, then the room fills with bright, clean daylight. The same antique mirror now stands in a bright white, minimal room. Calm, airy, fresh.
- **Must:** a clear switch from dark to bright. This is the change from confused to clear.

### Scene 6 – The ask (0:10–0:12, 2s)
- **Voiceover:** "Comment 'mirror.'" *(optional loop: "Comment 'mirror.' It wasn't you.")*
- **On-screen text:** COMMENT "MIRROR" 🎃
- **Video prompt:**
  > The woman smiles at the camera in the bright white room and points down toward the bottom of the screen. The antique mirror behind her. Warm, friendly, confident.
- **Add in editor:** `cover.png` showing in the mirror behind her.

## 3. On-screen text rules

- **Six words or fewer per card.** Most people watch muted.
- **Safe zone:** keep all text in the middle of the frame. Nothing in the top ~230px or bottom ~480px, and keep the right 230px clear (Instagram's buttons and caption sit there).
- **Style:** bold serif capitals, a thin pink bar on the left like the cover.
  - Scenes 1–4: **white** text on dark.
  - Scenes 5–6: **black** text on white.
  - One key word per card in pink.
- **Turn on auto-captions** for the voiceover as well, in a small clean font, in the safe zone.

---

## 4. Final checklist before export

- [ ] Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, ~11–12s
- [ ] Movement in the very first frame
- [ ] No scene longer than about 3 seconds without a visual change
- [ ] All text spelled correctly (re-check anything the AI generated)
- [ ] Cover appears in scenes 4–6
- [ ] Clear switch from dark to bright at scene 5
- [ ] "MIRROR" said out loud and shown on screen
- [ ] Faces and hands look natural (regenerate any scene that looks off)
- [ ] Post by **Oct 30–31** while the Halloween theme is current
- [ ] Use the caption and DM sequence from [README.md](README.md)

## Using yourself instead of an AI presenter

If you'd rather be on camera: film scenes 1, 2 and 6 yourself on your phone, and use AI only for the mirror effects in scenes 3–5. Real faces build more trust with a warm audience, and your own voice works better than an AI one.
