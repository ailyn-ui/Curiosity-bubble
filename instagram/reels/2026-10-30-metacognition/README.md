# Metacognition Reel (draft)

Explains metacognition so an ADHD woman founder feels why it matters: noticing what
your brain is doing, while it's doing it, turns the aftermath and the shame into a
pause, and the pause into a choice. Leads to the Know Yourself Journal (a series of
reflective questions); the Reel only makes people curious and doesn't describe it.

```
REEL READY
hook:       #20 Permission, "You don't need more willpower. You need to catch your brain in the act." 72 STRONG
length:     ~38s across 15 beats at 165 wpm, loops on "willpower"
on-screen:  10 cards
humanizer:  0 AI tells; human score 50 REVIEW (short spoken lines read as uniform)
caption:    run /ig-caption next
```

## Script

```
You don't need more willpower.
You need to catch your brain in the act.
That's metacognition.
Noticing what your brain is doing, while it's doing it.
With ADHD, everything moves fast.
4 hours vanish into a logo.
One email sends you spiralling.
By 5, you're snapping at everyone.
Without noticing, all you get is the aftermath. And the shame.
Noticing gives you a pause.
"Oh. I'm hyperfocusing." "I'm overstimulated."
That pause is where you get to choose.
Write it down, and you start seeing your patterns.
Want to know what your patterns are? Comment "journal".
Less willpower. More catching your brain in the act.
```
