# Listening conversations - invitations (2026-11)

Goal: 10 conversations with women founders who have **more ideas than
capacity** and **use AI for their business**. Tests positioning v2
(`positioning.md`). Listening only: no pitch.

Where: women-founder groups, ADHD-business communities, "AI for business"
communities, LinkedIn, Ailyn's own network and introductions.
Not: cold DMs to strangers on Instagram (lowest yield, and risks the account).

Every message is sent by hand.

---

```
Hi everyone, I'm Ailyn, a brand strategist in Zurich, and a mom with an ADHD brain.

I'm looking for 10 women founders who'd tell me how things really are in their business right now. Especially if you have more ideas than time, and you've started using AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Canva, anything) to keep up.

There's no pitch in these conversations. I'm not selling anything. I want to understand what it's like before I build what comes next.

It takes about 20 minutes, on a call or by voice notes, whatever's easier. As a thank-you, I'll send you The Message Mirror, a short exercise I made for seeing your offer the way your clients see it.

If that's you, comment or message me and I'll send the questions.
```

## 3. Reply when someone says yes

```
Thank you, {name}! Here are the questions. Answer whenever it suits you, by voice note or in writing, all at once or in pieces. Nothing needs to be polished.

If you'd rather talk, we can do a 20-minute call instead. Just tell me what works.
```

Then send the questions (section 4).

## 4. The questions

```
Before we start: in a sentence, what's your business, and how long have you been at it?

1. Think of the last time you tried to fix how your business is seen or explained. A new website, a rebrand, new words for your offer. What happened?
2. What was going on that made it "now"?
3. What did you try, and what did it cost you, in money and in time?
4. How are you using AI for this today? Where has it helped, and where has it fallen short?
5. What almost stopped you, or is stopping you now?
6. If this felt sorted, what would a normal Tuesday look like for you?
7. Is there someone else you think I should talk to?
```

## 5. Follow-ups (only for people who said yes and haven't answered)

**+4 days.** Add something new. Only send it if you have a real thing to share.

```
Hi {name}, one thing I'm noticing from the first answers: {something real you've heard, no names}. Curious if that's true for you too.

No rush at all. A voice note from the car counts.
```

**+10 days.** Close the loop, with a real date. Then stop.

```
Hi {name}, I'm closing this round on {date}. If now isn't the time, that's completely fine, and thank you for saying yes. I'll still send you the Mirror.
```
