# The Message Mirror - A Tool for Clarity

Free 14-page printable PDF by Ailyn Eida (© 2026). Shared for personal,
non-commercial use; workshops, programmes or client use need Ailyn's written
permission.

## What it is

A pen-and-paper self-reflection exercise on **one offer** the founder sells
now. 10 to 30 minutes ("Q4 is worth extra time"). Nothing to solve, just look.

- **Side one** (Shaw: "The single biggest problem in communication is the
  illusion that it has taken place.")
  1. What am I asking clients to buy? (in your own words)
  2. What value do I believe I create? (what changes, what's hard to see
     before working with you)
  3. What am I actually telling people? (open your page / offer doc and
     notice, don't rewrite)
- **Side two** (Anaïs Nin: "We don't see things as they are, we see them as
  we are.")
  4. What do my clients think they're buying? (real questions, mismatched
     expectations, complaints, surprise value, thanks; bring in real messages;
     separate what clients said from what you assume)
- **Side by side** (Sylvia Plath, "Mirror") - look at all four answers
  together: what stands out?
- **What's your takeaway?** "One thing I see differently about my offer now is..."
- **The door is now open.** "Found a gap? That's the first step of what I build
  with clients." Keep it, come back, share with someone you trust. DM or email
  Ailyn what stood out: "I'll share my perspective. No pitch."
